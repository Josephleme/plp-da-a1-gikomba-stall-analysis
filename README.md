# Wanjiku's Gikomba Market Stall Analysis

## Wanjiku's Business Problem
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and requires data-driven clarity regarding her operations, financial direction, and inventory management. Over a 14-day cycle, she tracked her daily revenue streams alongside stock volumes, but lacked the structured tools necessary to convert these records into prescriptive business strategies. As a freelance data analyst, I applied the core analytics lifecycle to analyze her KES 44,720.00 total revenue footprint, diagnose severe weekend-to-weekday customer traffic disparities, isolate margin yields across different items, and identify critical inventory reorder risks to protect her from future stockouts.

## Top Three Findings

**1. Massive Weekend Performance:** Wanjiku's average weekend revenue (KES 4,900.00) is **79.82% higher** than her regular weekday revenue average (KES 2,725.00), showing that her consumer base is heavily concentrated on Saturdays and Sundays.
**2. Positive Growth Trajectory:** The business exhibits strong upward momentum, registering a **10.45% Week-on-Week growth rate** as total revenue climbed from KES 21,250.00 in Week 1 to KES 23,470.00 in Week 2.
**3. Critical Stock Depletion Risks:** An inventory audit revealed that **Tomatoes** (18kg remaining vs 25kg reorder trigger) and **Sukuma** (9kg remaining vs 15kg reorder trigger) have breached their minimum safety thresholds and require immediate reordering to prevent costly stockouts.
